How to Upload Video to TikTok for High Quality

Creator Masterclass · Obito Studio Video Optimization

This masterclass outlines the exact four-phase workflow required to upload 4K, 60fps, 120fps, and 10-bit HDR videos to TikTok with zero generational quality loss.

Phase 01 · Video Editor Export

Export at Your Preferred Resolution & Bitrate

Supported editors: DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, CapCut, After Effects, Final Cut Pro.

  • Recommended Bitrate: ~30 Mbps (30,000 kbps) for clean master detail. Higher or lower bitrates are 100% accepted by Obito Studio.
  • Frame Rate: 60 fps or 120 fps native fluidity. TikTok's server heuristics often downsample non-standard container clocks to 30fps unless patched.
  • Color & HDR: Enable HDR (HLG / Rec.2020) in your editor if you desire punchy dynamic range in the TikTok feed.
Phase 02 · Crucial Mobile Rule

Save & Upload From "Files App" (Not Photos/Gallery)

When transferring or selecting your video on iOS or Android, ALWAYS pick your video from your mobile Files App (iCloud / Internal Storage) instead of the Photos/Gallery picker.

  • Why Files App? Mobile photo galleries silently downsample high-bitrate video, strip 10-bit HDR down to 8-bit SDR, and drop frame rates before web upload.
  • The Rule: Selecting via "Choose File → Browse / Files" preserves 100% of your master bitrate, frame count, and color gamut.
Phase 03 · Obito Studio Remux

Optimize Container Atoms With Zero Generational Loss

Upload your master export to Obito Studio. The engine performs 100% bit-for-bit stream copy (-c:v copy).

  • FPS + Quality + HDR: Translates Apple Dolby Vision Profile 8.4 dynamic RPU into native HLG Rec.2020 while locking 60/120fps.
  • FPS + Quality: Pure stream copy for standard SDR high-framerate clips.
  • Under the Hood: Zero pixel re-encoding. Container atom clocks are harmonized to 19200 timescale and dual AAC audio streams are preserved.
Phase 04 · Final Publishing

Upload via TikTok Desktop/Web for Maximum Bitrate

Once your master video has been processed by Obito Studio, publish directly to TikTok.

  • Direct Public Post: Publish directly as Public. Avoid saving as a "Private Draft" first, which triggers TikTok's aggressive secondary re-compression pass.
  • TikTok Web Uploader: Using tiktok.com/upload on your mobile or desktop browser bypasses mobile client-side compression rungs and honors high-framerate container atoms.
