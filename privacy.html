Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Summary

Vague Transcode runs entirely on your own computer. It does not operate a server, does not collect analytics, and does not transmit your videos anywhere except to TikTok, when you explicitly ask it to.

Information we process

  • Video files. Read from your computer and processed locally. They are never uploaded to any service operated by us — we do not operate one.
  • TikTok access tokens. If you connect a TikTok account, the access and refresh tokens are stored only on your computer, in ~/.vague/tiktok.json, with file permissions restricted to your user account. They are never transmitted to us.
  • No analytics. No telemetry, tracking, cookies, advertising identifiers or usage reporting of any kind.

Data sent to TikTok

When you choose to upload, the following is sent directly from your computer to TikTok's official API endpoints:

  • The video file you selected
  • Your TikTok access token, to authorise the upload to your own account

Uploads are sent to your TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published publicly by this tool. TikTok's handling of that data is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Your control

  • Revoke access at any time: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions
  • Delete stored tokens by removing the ~/.vague folder
  • Uninstalling the tool removes all of its data, because all of it is local

Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date. For questions about this policy, contact the maintainer through the repository linked from the home page.
