Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Obito Studio ("we", "us") is a web tool at obitostudio.willsonrai.com.np that repackages videos so that high frame rate and HDR survive TikTok's upload processing, and optionally posts the result to the creator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data we handle and why. We keep it short on purpose; if anything is unclear, contact us (see bottom).

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies or sell any data.

2. Posting to TikTok

If you click Post to TikTok, you are sent to TikTok to sign in and approve Obito Studio. TikTok then gives us:

  • your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic) – shown in the posting form so you can see which account you're posting to;
  • an access token that lets us upload a video to your account (scopes video.upload and/or video.publish).

What we do with it:

  • We upload only the video you just optimized, only when you press the Post button, with the title, privacy level and comment/duet/stitch settings you chose. We never post, like, follow, comment or read your messages, followers or other videos.
  • Tokens are stored encrypted (AES‑256‑GCM) on our server and are used solely to post on your behalf and to refresh the token when it expires.
  • We do not store copies of your TikTok content, drafts or statistics.

Disconnecting: use the "Disconnect" link in the TikTok posting form – this deletes your token from our server immediately. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Either action stops all further access.

Your use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

3. Who can see your data

  • Nobody but us and the services needed to run the site. Videos are processed on our own server. When you post to TikTok, the video and your chosen settings are transmitted to TikTok (ByteDance), which then processes them under its own policies.
  • We do not share, sell or rent personal data. We disclose data only if required by law.

4. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. TikTok tokens are encrypted at rest. Uploaded files are stored in a private directory, never at a public URL, and are deleted on schedule. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you (your code, logs, TikTok connection) by contacting us. Videos are deleted automatically within about an hour, so there is usually nothing to request. If you are in the EU/UK you also have rights of access, rectification and portability, and the right to complain to your data‑protection authority.

6. Children

Obito Studio is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, for TikTok‑connected users, show a notice in the posting form.
