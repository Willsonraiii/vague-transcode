What it finds

✗Variable frame ratethe 60→30 killer

The single most common reason a 60fps export gets delivered at 30. Platforms re-time VFR footage. Read straight from the frame timing table, not guessed.

✗Broken HDR tagsthe grey look

Wide-gamut pixels labelled as SDR — the classic CapCut export fault. It's why HDR footage comes out washed out and milky.

✗Transfer damagealready too late

A 4K file that's 8-bit H.264 SDR was re-encoded by WhatsApp or by iOS "Automatic" transfer. That loss happened before you even opened this page.

What nobody else will tell you

Every platform re-encodes every upload. TikTok delivers about 1080p at 2–2.5 Mbps no matter what you send it. No tool prevents that.

Tools claiming "no compression" are describing the file they hand back to you — not what your viewers actually receive.

What you can control: the quality of the source their encoder works from, and whether your frame rate and colour survive the trip. That's this.

Need more than the container fixed?

Downscaling 4K to 1080p while keeping Dolby Vision can't be done in a browser — a canvas is SDR only, so any in-browser re-encode destroys HDR.

The desktop tool uses ffmpeg and dovi_tool to do it properly: extract the DV metadata, encode the base layer, inject it back, and verify it survived.
