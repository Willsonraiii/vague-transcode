What it checks

✓Variable frame ratethe 60→30 killer
VFR is the most common reason an exported 60 fps video is delivered at 30. Platforms re-time it. We detect it from the raw frame timing table.
✓HDR colour taggingwashed-out look
Wide-gamut pixels tagged as SDR is the classic CapCut export fault — it's why HDR footage looks grey and milky after upload. We read the actual colr box.

Honest limits

Every platform re-encodes every upload. TikTok delivers around 1080p at 2–2.5 Mbps no matter what you send. No tool changes that — anyone claiming "no compression" is describing the file they hand you, not what viewers receive.

What you can control is the source their encoder works from, and whether your frame rate and colour survive the trip. That's what this does.

Need to downscale 4K to 1080p while keeping Dolby Vision? That can't be done in a browser — a canvas is SDR only. Use the desktop tool for that.
